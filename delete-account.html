Delete your Walk Together account

App: Walk Together (com.walktog.app) Developer: Nikolay Koychev Български

In the app

  1. Open Walk Together and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm with Delete permanently.

Deletion happens immediately and can't be undone.

Without the app

Send an email to nikolay.koychev@gmail.com from the address you sign in with (your Google account), with the subject “Delete my account”. Requests are completed within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, profile photo, Revolut username.
  • Your step history.
  • Your group memberships and the groups you created.
  • Shared locations and push notification tokens.

We keep no data after deletion unless required by law. Data stored in Health Connect on your phone is not affected; you control it in Android system settings.
